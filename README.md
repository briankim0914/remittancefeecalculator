Bitcoin Remittance GDP Impact Calculator

A Python project designed to calculate the projected GDP percentage increase for top remittance-receiving countries if cross-border payments transition from traditional banking wire transfers to Bitcoin.

Overview

Traditional international remittance corridors often charge high transfer fees and foreign exchange margins, reducing the net capital received by individuals in developing economies. This calculator models the financial impact of replacing those legacy banking fees with Bitcoin network transaction costs. By retaining more capital within recipient countries, the tool calculates the resulting increase in effective national GDP for 36 top remittance-receiving nations.

Features

Models fee savings by comparing traditional bank wire/transfer costs against Bitcoin transaction fees.

Calculates projected percentage increases in national GDP based on capital retention across top remittance countries.

Processes and structures economic datasets using Python for direct analysis and visualization in Power BI.

Data & Methodology

Data Collection: Aggregated remittance inflows, traditional wire fee averages, and national GDP data for top recipient countries.

Savings Calculation: Evaluated net fee differences by shifting remittance flows to Bitcoin networks.

GDP Projection: Calculated the percentage expansion in national GDP resulting from retained capital flows.
